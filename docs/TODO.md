- [x] Whether `plc_station_upload` may answer a TLS challenge `Trusted` when it offers no certificate. Its identity comes from the ownership checks, not from a certificate. One certificate keeps the pin rule; more than one, no opt-in, or any other target still refuses.
- [x] Whether production Hosts may dispatch force-table document edits now that `local.watch.force-edits.20261029` qualifies them.
